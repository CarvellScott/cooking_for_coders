# Carvell's serious-fried rice
The joke of "you mean a chicken fried this rice" is getting old. I want a no-nonsense, hyper-optimized tasty fried rice, and this is my attempt at creating it so far.

## Info  

- **Prep time:** 3h
- **Cook time:** 16m
- **Servings:** 2

## Tools

- Zojirushi Rice Cooker

## Ingredients

- [ ] 2 zojirushi cups [enriched long grain white rice, cooked then refrigerated][enriched long grain white rice]
- [ ] 2 tbsp [seasame oil][seasame oil]
- [ ] 2 tbsp [soy sauce][soy sauce]
- [ ] 1 tsp [garlic powder][garlic powder]
- [ ] 1/2 [onion, diced and frozen][onion]
- [ ] 1 cup [peas and carots, frozen][peas and corrots]
- [ ] 2 [eggs, whisked][eggs]
- [ ] 1 [chicken breast, diced][chicken]

## Steps

1. Prepare the rice by following whatever rice cooker instructions, then toss it in the fridge to cool.
1. Combine everything except the chicken and eggs. Stir, shake, do whatever you can to get a uniform mixture.
2. Add the rice mixture to the air fryer basket without using a liner or the stand.
3. Add sliced chicken breast on top of the rice mixture, spread evenlyish throughout.
4. Air Fry for 5 minutes at 400 °F. Stir.
5. Air fry for another 5 minutes at 400 °F.
6. Make some free space in the air fryer for the eggs, leaving about a quarter of the area free.
7. Add the eggs. Air fry for 6 more minutes at 400 °F.
8. Break the eggs into smaller pieces and mix them into the rest of the rice.

## Notes  

- I use a rice cooker for my rice. The majority of the recipe is determined by how you cook it.
- Version 1: I had followed standard zojirushi instructions for the rice, but it kept ending up extra sticky and more of a paste. Added sliced microwavable chicken breast after. Full of burnt parts after air frying, but even after removing them, kinda tasted like slop.
- Version 2: Rice still paste. Cooked chicken breast in the air fryer then added it to the rice. Did not remove burnt parts but at least was edible enough that teriyaki sauce saved it.
- Version 3: I used slightly less water, I'm no longer sure what ratio I used, but the texture was much better. Realized my seasame oil is expired.
- Version 4: No longer using the Silicone air fryer liner or the small stand in it. Raw sliced chicken breast now added on top of the rice as it cooks in the air fryer, with both cooking concurrently. Everything else touches the basket surface. Somehow the burnt parts started tasting good? Or like they were grilled.
- Version 5 I had tried to use a ratio of 2:3 rice:water where Zojirushi recommends 1:1. HEB's instructions recommend 1:2. Made way too much rice, and tasted burnt throughout, but not as good as previously.
- Version 6 (WIP): Used Zoshirushi's 1:1 ratio, BUT I added the rice first THEN filled to the water level; I had this reversed previously. The rice came out successfully fluffy. This should be the last of the expired seasame oil.
- Version 7 (Future): Need to calculate a deterministic time. First fry is between 5-6 minutes, second is 4-5 minutes, final is 1-6 minutes, depending on chicken status. Total is 11-16 minutes.
