# Foreword

For my Mom, Maurica Scott, whose love, faith, and inspiration made me into the man I am today.

This is a compilation of my Mom's favorite recipes. It started as an attempt at making a recipe book, then started to evolve into a web page before I realized the complexity of trying to turn it into a single-page offline-only page was competing with the complexity of establishing consistency between these recipes. In the end, simplicity won out and I went with something Mom could laminate. :)

This labor of love was made with pandoc, html5, and css3. I tell ya, the web version's better with a fancy menu that jumps to individual recipes, but I gotta work on figuring out a means of privately distributing a .html file for that to work...
