# Yogurt Bread

This is one of the first recipes I learned that is "actual cooking" but it's still easy enough that it's hard to screw up.

## Info  

- **Prep time:** 5m
- **Cook time:** 40m
- **Servings:** 10

## Tools

- 8x4 in. loaf pan
- Large Mixing bowl

## Ingredients

- [ ] 3 cups [self-rising flour][self_rising_flour]
- [ ] 1.75 cups [low fat vanilla yogurt][low_fat_vanilla_yogurt]

## Steps  

1. Preheat your oven to 375 ° F. Line an 8x4 inch loaf pan with parchment paper.
2. Mix the flour and yogurt in a large bowl until the flour is completely incorporated. This is easily the longest step.
3. Pour the batter into the 8x4 inch loaf pan and bake for 35 minutes.
4. Remove the pan from the oven and let the bread sit in the pan for an additional 5 minutes before transferring it to a wire rack to fully-cool.

## Notes  

- Making sure the flour and yogurt mix together is the most important part. Otherwise when you bake the bread, the center will likely still be moist and/or gooey.
